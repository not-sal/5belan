5belan for macOS (11 or newer, Apple Silicon or Intel)

1. Unzip this folder.
2. Double-click "Install 5belan.command". The first time macOS may block it:
   right-click it > Open > Open. (The app is not signed with an Apple developer certificate.)
3. The installer needs Python 3.9 - 3.14 (python.org or Homebrew) and an internet connection; it takes a few minutes.
4. Open 5belan from ~/Applications. First start: open Settings (bottom of the sidebar) and paste your DeepSeek API key.

Updates: the app checks for updates by itself and shows a banner at the top of the chat.
Your chats and settings are kept in ~/Library/Application Support/5belan and are not touched by updates or reinstalls (chats from an older install are imported automatically on first start).
If the window does not open, read launch.log in that folder.
